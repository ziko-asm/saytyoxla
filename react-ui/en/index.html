Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

Yes. The check is completely free and needs no sign-up.

How do I know if my website is secure?

Enter your website address in the box above and press “Check”. SaytYoxla checks the HTTPS connection, the SSL certificate, HSTS, CSP and other protective headers, whether the server version is exposed, and mixed (http) content, and explains the fix for every problem in plain words.

How can I check when my SSL certificate expires?

After the check, the “Security” section shows how many days the certificate is still valid and its expiry date. When a certificate expires, browsers mark the site as “not secure”, so renew it in time — ideally turn on automatic renewal (for example, Let's Encrypt).

What are SPF and DMARC, and why do they matter?

SPF and DMARC are records in your domain's DNS settings. They say which servers may send e-mail in your name. Without them, scammers can write fake e-mails that look like they came from your domain. SaytYoxla checks both; if they are missing, ask your DNS or hosting provider to add them.

Why doesn't my website show up on Google?

The most common reasons: the page title and meta description are missing or weak, the site is not mobile-friendly, or the site was never submitted to Google. SaytYoxla checks the title, the description and mobile-friendliness. A new site should also be added to Google Search Console with a sitemap.

Why does my website look wrong on a phone?

Usually the page has no “viewport” tag, so the phone opens the site at desktop size and shrinks it. SaytYoxla checks this in the “Mobile and Google” section and shows the tag to add.

Can the check harm my website?

No. We only look at what is open to everyone: we open the home page, the files it loads (scripts, styles, images), robots.txt and sitemap.xml once and read the certificate and public DNS records. We change nothing and try to break nothing. Exposed files and ports are looked at only in the deep check, after the site's owner has confirmed it.

Is my data stored?

We do not ask for your name or phone, and there are no cookies or tracking scripts. An e-mail address is needed only if you choose ongoing watching of your site yourself, and it is deleted when you stop the watching. The checked address and its result stay for up to 10 minutes to speed up a repeat check, and your IP address stays for up to 1 day in the counter that limits abuse.

What does the score mean?

100 is the best result. 90+ is excellent (A), 75+ good (B), 60+ fair (C), 40+ weak (D), below 40 needs serious attention (F). Red problems lower the score the most. Missing basic protection limits the score: no more than 39 without trusted HTTPS, 59 if the certificate expires in under 7 days, 74 with 4–5 basic settings missing and 89 with 2–3 missing.
